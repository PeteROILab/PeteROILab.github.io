Privacy Policy

Effective: November 23, 2026

Who we are

Workflow ROI Lab is an educational YouTube channel. The Workflow ROI Lab Uploader is a private automation tool operated for the channel owner's own authorized YouTube account. This website provides information about the channel and that tool; it does not offer public accounts or Google sign-in to visitors.

Information accessed through Google

When the owner authorizes the uploader, it requests YouTube permissions necessary to identify the authorized channel, upload videos, schedule and manage approved uploads, read publishing state, and collect basic performance or status information. The tool only acts on the account explicitly authorized by the owner.

Use and disclosure

Information obtained through Google APIs is used solely to operate, audit, and improve the owner's Workflow ROI Lab YouTube production and publishing workflow. We do not sell Google user data or provide it to advertising data brokers. Access is limited to the owner's authorized automation and its necessary service providers, including Google/YouTube and GitHub Actions used to run the workflow.

Credentials and retention

Authorization credentials are kept in restricted local configuration and/or encrypted GitHub Actions secrets, not embedded in this public website or repository. Operational logs and release records may retain video IDs, timestamps, nonsecret fingerprints, publishing status, and related audit evidence. Access may be revoked from the Google Account's third-party access settings; related automation access will then stop. The owner may request deletion of retained workflow data via the contact address below, subject to necessary security and audit retention.

Visitors and third-party hosting

This static website does not have forms, cookies set by our own code, visitor accounts, or embedded trackers. The site host and linked services may process standard access logs or other data under their own policies. Following the YouTube link takes visitors to Google's services and their privacy practices.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Workflow ROI Lab Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements where applicable.
